Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Waha (bundle ID com.wahaboulderdm.kpvr), published by wahaboulderdm, handles information when you use the iOS app. It covers the climber account, projects and beta, rallies, messages, photos that may show a face, notifications, Holds, and community reports.

The Terms of Service explain the rules for using those features. This policy explains the information those features use.

1. Who we are

Publisher: wahaboulderdm
App: Waha
Bundle ID: com.wahaboulderdm.kpvr
Contact: waha.crew.kpvr@gmail.com

Waha does not sell personal information.

2. Information Waha uses

2.1 Account and session

Quick Login creates a climber ID and a password and starts a session so you can open the app without typing those details again. Log Out ends the session and returns you to the login screen. The climber ID remains so you can sign in again.

Delete Account asks you to confirm. The confirmation states that ticklists, direct beta, and grade progression linked to the account will be removed. Confirming signs you out and returns you to the login screen.

The login screen does not collect an Apple ID, a Google account, or a phone number for sign-in.

2.2 Profile

The profile and the edit-profile screen use a display name, a climbing intent, a grade, a photo, and project summaries. Settings can show an email line and a phone line as part of the account card. Visibility switches (stealth, who can see activity, and whether a ready beacon is public) record the choice you make on that screen.

2.3 Projects, attempts, and topo

Waha uses the routes on your board, the grade, the gym or crag name, attempt notes, the progress step you select, and tags you attach to an attempt. The topo tool uses the photo on the canvas and the marks and ink you add while that screen is open.

2.4 Map and gyms

The map shows gym and boulder locations on Apple Maps, plus names, areas, and session notes for those places. If you mark yourself ready or open a rally from the map, Waha uses that action to show the related session.

Apple's map service receives the map request needed to draw the base map, under Apple's own terms.

2.5 Rallies

A rally uses the session title, time, place, host name, roster count, spots open, and the gear points shown on the join confirmation. Confirming a join records that you accepted those points.

2.6 Feed, beta, and messages

The feed, beta discussion, and crew chat use the text you write, the clip or topo attached to a post, the climber name on the post, and messages in a thread. A beta page can also show a short audio note and gift activity under the clip.

2.7 Holds and gifts

Waha uses your Holds balance, the pack you select in the wallet, the price shown for that pack, wallet history lines, and the gift you choose to send, including its Holds cost and the climber you send it to.

2.8 Notifications

The first time you open the Projects home, iOS asks whether Waha may send alerts, badges, and sounds. Waha stores the fact that it has already asked, so it does not ask on every launch. Settings also has preference switches for beta alerts, rally invites, and grade alerts. Those switches record the choice on that screen.

2.9 Support mail

If you email waha.crew.kpvr@gmail.com, we receive whatever you put in that message, including your return address.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • sign you in and return you to the login screen when you log out or delete the account;
  • show your projects, routes, attempts, topo marks, and profile;
  • show gyms, the map, and rallies, and record a join confirmation;
  • show the feed, beta discussion, and crew messages;
  • show your Holds balance, wallet history, and gifts;
  • ask once for notification permission and respect the preference switches in Settings;
  • review a community report and remove content or end access when the Terms require it;
  • respond to you if you email us about a purchase or these policies.

We do not use your information to build a biometric face profile. We do not use it for cross-app advertising. Waha does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

4. Permissions

Notifications. The system permission dialog appears the first time you reach the Projects home. Waha requests alerts, badge, and sound. The purpose is beta and rally alerts. There is no second button elsewhere in the app that asks for notifications again. If you decline, you can still use the rest of Waha. You can change the iOS choice later in iOS Settings.

Camera, Microphone, and Photo Library. This version of Waha does not request those system permissions. Climbing photos shown on routes, profiles, and the feed are part of that content. The attempt screen includes a control labeled for a beta clip; it does not open the camera or the photo library in this version.

Tracking. Waha does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps.

5. Facial data

This chapter is the explanation of how Waha collects, uses, discloses, shares, and retains facial data, for App Store Guideline 2.1 and 5.1.1.

5.1 What is collected

Waha does not collect biometric faceprints, face geometry, or a facial recognition template.

Profile photos, crew photos, and beta stills may depict a climber's face. Those images are ordinary photos, not a biometric identifier. Waha does not measure a face, does not create a face template, and does not compare a face against a database of faces.

5.2 How facial images are used

Photos that show a face are used to display a climber's identity and climbing media inside Waha: profile, feed, gifts, and crew conversation. Waha does not use them to identify you across other companies' apps. Waha does not use them as a login method.

5.3 Disclosure and sharing

A photo that shows a face can be seen by other climbers on the profile, feed, and crew surfaces where that photo is posted. Waha does not sell these photos. Waha does not send them to a third-party artificial intelligence provider. Waha does not give them to advertising networks.

5.4 Where facial images are kept, and for how long

Facial images that are part of a climber profile or post are kept with that account's community content. They remain until the account is deleted or the content is removed after a community report. Confirming Delete Account signs you out; the deletion confirmation states that beta videos and progression linked to the account are removed.

A copy of a photo may remain in an email you sent us as part of a report, for as long as we need that email to finish the review and the 24-hour action described in the Terms.

5.5 This chapter

Section 5, including §§5.1–5.5, is Waha's explanation of the collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial data.

6. Third-party artificial intelligence

Waha does not use a third-party AI service to generate or analyze your content. Waha does not send your prompts, reference images, photos, messages, or account data to a third-party AI provider.

No prompt is sent. No reference image is sent. No account data is sent for AI generation or AI analysis.

7. Community reports

If you report inappropriate activity, email waha.crew.kpvr@gmail.com. The message can include the climber name, the screen, a description of the content, the time, and your email address. We use that message to review the report, remove the content, and end the offending user's access within 24 hours of a report we can review.

That email is delivered by your mail provider. We do not add a separate advertising use for report mail.

8. Holds and Apple payments

Top-ups are Apple In-App Purchases. Apple processes the payment and applies Apple's privacy policy to the payment itself. Waha does not receive or store your card number.

Waha keeps the Holds balance, the pack shown in the wallet, and the gift amount. Holds do not expire and have no cash value. Prices shown in the app are in U.S. dollars for the five packs listed in the Terms.

9. Who else receives information

  • Apple, when you buy a Holds pack, when the map draws, and when iOS presents the notification permission.
  • Your mail provider and wahaboulderdm, when you email a report or a support question.
  • Other climbers, when you post, message, join a rally, or show a profile photo in those community surfaces.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertising brokers.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to respond to a valid legal request, limited to what the request covers.

10. Retention

We keep account and community information for as long as the account is in use, so the projects, feed, messages, and wallet can function.

Log Out keeps the climber ID so you can sign in again. Delete Account signs you out after a confirmation that describes removal of ticklists, beta, and progression.

Report emails are kept long enough to complete the review and the 24-hour action, and longer if a dispute about that report is still open.

11. Children

Waha is not for anyone under 18. Community posting, messaging, rallies, and gifts are limited to people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used Waha, email waha.crew.kpvr@gmail.com and we will delete the account access associated with that report.

12. Your choices

  • You can refuse notifications on the system dialog, or change that choice later in iOS Settings.
  • You can turn the notification preference switches off in Waha Settings.
  • You can log out.
  • You can delete the account from Settings after confirming the warning.
  • You can email waha.crew.kpvr@gmail.com to ask what this policy covers or to send a community report.

13. International use

Waha is operated by wahaboulderdm and is offered in English. If you use Waha from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be processed in the places where we and Apple operate the features described in this policy.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top. The current policy is linked under Quick Login, in Settings, and in the Holds wallet. If you keep using Waha after an update, the updated policy applies to information we handle after that date.
