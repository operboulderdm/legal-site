Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Waha (bundle ID com.wahaboulderdm.kpvr), published by wahaboulderdm, handles information. It covers the climber account, Holds, notifications, photos that may show a face, and community reports.

1. Who we are

Publisher: wahaboulderdm
Contact: waha.crew.kpvr@gmail.com

2. Information Waha uses

  • Account. Quick Login creates a climber ID and password and keeps a session so you can return to Waha. Log Out ends the session. Delete Account asks you to confirm, then signs you out.
  • Profile and community content. Display name, climbing grade, project notes, beta posts, crew messages, rally details, and photos you see on profiles, the feed, and in chat.
  • Holds. Your Holds balance, the pack you select, and gift amounts shown in the wallet.
  • Settings. Switches you change for stealth, feed visibility, and notification preferences.
  • Notification permission. The choice you make when iOS asks for notifications.

Waha does not sell personal information.

3. Permissions

Notifications. The first time you open the Projects home, iOS asks for notification permission (alerts, badge, and sound). Waha uses that permission for beta and rally alerts. There is no separate button elsewhere in Waha that asks for notifications again. Waha does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Waha does not request Camera, Microphone, or Photo Library permission in this version. Climbing photos shown in the product are part of route, profile, and beta content.

4. Facial data

This chapter is the explanation of how Waha collects, uses, discloses, shares, and retains facial data, for App Store Guideline 2.1 and 5.1.1.

4.1 What is collected

Waha does not collect biometric faceprints, face geometry, or a facial recognition template.

Profile photos, crew photos, and beta stills may depict a climber's face. Those images are ordinary photos, not a biometric identifier.

4.2 How facial images are used

Photos that show a face are used to display a climber's identity and climbing media inside Waha: profile, feed, gifts, and crew conversation. Waha does not use them to identify you across other companies' apps.

4.3 Disclosure and sharing

A photo that shows a face can be seen by other climbers on the profile, feed, and crew surfaces where that photo is posted. Waha does not sell these photos. Waha does not send them to a third-party artificial intelligence provider.

4.4 Where facial images are kept, and for how long

Facial images that are part of a climber profile or post are kept with that account's community content. They remain until the account is deleted or the content is removed after a community report. Confirming Delete Account signs you out; the deletion confirmation states that beta videos and progression linked to the account are removed.

4.5 This chapter

Section 4, including §§4.1–4.5, is Waha's explanation of the collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial data.

5. Third-party artificial intelligence

Waha does not use a third-party AI service to generate or analyze your content. Waha does not send your prompts, reference images, photos, messages, or account data to a third-party AI provider.

6. Community reports

If you report inappropriate activity, email waha.crew.kpvr@gmail.com. The message you send can include the climber name, the screen, a description of the content, and your email address. We use that message to review the report, remove the content, and end the offending user's access within 24 hours of a report we can review. The email leaves your mailbox through your mail provider.

7. Holds purchases

Top-ups are Apple In-App Purchases. Apple processes the payment. Waha does not receive or store your card number. Waha keeps the Holds balance and the pack shown in your wallet. Holds do not expire and have no cash value.

8. Children

Waha is not for anyone under 18. Community posting is limited to people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. The current policy is linked from the login screen and from Settings.
